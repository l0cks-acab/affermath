# Changelog

## 0.4.0
- Affermath is now a GroundBurst addon and requires GroundBurst 0.11.0+ (and GeckoLib).
- New rare structure: a derelict nuclear power plant. Two hyperboloid cooling towers (one broken open), a breached reactor dome over a flooded pit of cooled corium, a turbine hall, a control block with loot and a soldier spawner, a flue stack, overhead pipes and a fenced concrete apron. Find it with /locate structure affermath:power_plant.
- The plant is properly hot: GroundBurst cooled corium, waste drums, irradiated soil and fallout ash.
- Toxic sludge is radioactive under GroundBurst's radiation system, on top of Fallout Sickness.
- GroundBurst hazmat hoods (and lead-lined ones) work as a gas mask: no sludge sickness and further sight in the fog.
- GroundBurst steel, lead, circuits, chips, uranium, fuel rods, anti-rad meds and hazmat gear now turn up in ruin, bunker and vault chests.

## 0.3.1
- Old highways: a loose grid of winding two-lane roads crosses the dimension, graded through the hills, with cracked asphalt, potholes, crumbling verges and a faded centre line. New blocks: Asphalt, Cracked Asphalt, Asphalt Road Line.
- Car wrecks now only turn up on roads, sitting in a lane, sometimes in little pile-ups.
- Roadside ruins: gutted concrete apartment blocks (balconies, rusted wall units, ladder core), two-storey brick shopfronts with charred awnings, and patchwork scrap shacks on stilts. Each is built fresh so no two match, and each has a ruin loot chest.
- Builders can drop one with /place feature affermath:ruined_apartment, ruined_shop or stilt_shack.

## 0.3.0
- Wasteland worldgen: scrap heaps, rusted car wrecks (some half-sunk or overturned), collapsed radio masts and toxic sludge puddles.
- Toxic Sludge slows you and gives Fallout Sickness (slow damage and hunger, worse the longer you wade). A gas mask keeps you safe.
- New blocks: Rusted Plating, Scrap Pile, Burnt Tire, Toxic Sludge. Scrap drops from scrap piles and smelts into iron nuggets.
- Stairs, slabs and walls for scorched bricks, scorched concrete and their cracked variants (crafting and stonecutter).
- Charred stairs, slab, fence, fence gate, door and trapdoor.
- Sound: howling wind, low drones, distant shelling, creaking steel and far-off gunfire across the dimension.
- Every mob has its own voice: Hollow Soldier groans, Ash Wraith whispers, Cinder Crow caws and the Cratomancer growls.

## 0.2.1
- Ash and ashen soil floors now pick from several texture variants at random, each randomly rotated, so the ground no longer tiles.
- Soil variants: dried mud cracks, a scorched patch, buried shrapnel and bone, a fallen burnt branch, rotted leaves with a charred root, cracked hardpan with a dead tuft, and rusted tin and scrap.
- Ash variants: soot-filled hollows, a cinder cluster, a fresh pale fall, and a charred stick half buried.
- Ash layers use the same variants.

## 0.2.0
- Retextured every block and item: small hand-picked colour ramps per material, cool shadows and warm highlights, drawn shapes instead of noise, outlined and lit item sprites.
- New custom models for all four mobs:
  - Hollow Soldier: steel helmet, gas mask with glowing lenses and a swinging filter, backpack, bedroll and greatcoat skirts.
  - Ash Wraith: hooded skull with pinprick eyes, long bone arms with claws, a trailing three-piece robe tail.
  - Cinder Crow: two-piece wings that fold and spread, fan tail, ember eyes and smouldering underside.
  - The Cratomancer: hunched iron hulk with exhaust stacks, a shoulder mortar, glowing furnace core, huge gauntlets and an overhead hammer attack.
- Glowing eyes and embers on all mobs show through the fog.
- The Cratomancer's stacks belch smoke and sparks, heavier in later phases.

## 0.1.0
- First release: Affermath dimension, portal, ruins, bunkers, Hollow Soldier, Ash Wraith, Cinder Crow, the Cratomancer, Warforged gear, Gas Mask, Dog Tags, Shrapnel Charm, Field Lantern, Ration Tin.
